DURATION: 11:38

AGENT: Good afternoon, Meridian Bank wealth services, this is James speaking. How can I help?

CUSTOMER: Hi James. I've got about fifteen thousand pounds sitting in my current account not doing much and I was thinking about investing it. Someone mentioned your Premier Growth Fund?

AGENT: Great timing actually — the Premier Growth Fund is one of our most popular products right now. It's been performing really well.

CUSTOMER: What kind of returns are we talking about?

AGENT: We've seen around nine to eleven percent annually over the last two years. It's a fantastic product.

CUSTOMER: That sounds impressive. Is it safe?

AGENT: Absolutely. It's a managed fund — our professional team handles everything. You don't need to worry about a thing. It's basically risk-free for someone in your position.

CUSTOMER: Risk-free? That does sound good. I'm 61 so I can't really afford to lose this money — it's part of my retirement pot.

AGENT: Completely understandable. This is perfect for someone approaching retirement. The fund is designed for capital preservation as well as growth.

CUSTOMER: What's the minimum I need to put in?

AGENT: Five thousand, but I'd say fifteen thousand is a great amount to start. You'll see the returns much more clearly.

CUSTOMER: What happens if I need the money back? Can I get it out quickly?

AGENT: You can request a withdrawal at any time. It usually takes about ten working days to process.

CUSTOMER: And there are no catches? No fees I should know about?

AGENT: There's a small annual management fee of one point two percent. That's very standard in the industry.

CUSTOMER: What about the cooling-off period? I think I'm entitled to one?

AGENT: There's a standard process for any changes you want to make after signing up.

CUSTOMER: Okay. Can I do this over the phone today?

AGENT: Absolutely, I can set that up for you right now. I just need to confirm your account details and we can get the application processed today.

CUSTOMER: Let's do it. Fifteen thousand.

AGENT: Perfect. Let me pull up your account. Just to confirm — you're happy to invest fifteen thousand pounds in the Premier Growth Fund?

CUSTOMER: Yes I think so. You've put my mind at ease. Risk-free and good returns — sounds ideal.

AGENT: Great. Let's proceed.
